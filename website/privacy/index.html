Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

Wotri collects only what it needs to let you rate food, share reviews and find good places to eat, and it never sells your data.

This policy covers the Wotri app for iPhone and Android and the services behind it. “Wotri”, “we” and “us” mean Dylan Wojtyna, who runs the app. By using Wotri you agree to this policy and our Terms of Use.

How we use your information

We use your information only to run and improve Wotri:

  • Create your account and keep you signed in
  • Show your profile, ratings, reviews, lists and photos
  • Show places near you and help you find the restaurant you’re at (location is used for the search and not saved to your profile)
  • Read dishes off menu photos you scan, so you can add them to a restaurant
  • Send in-app notifications when someone follows you, reacts to or comments on your review
  • Review reports, enforce our rules and keep the app safe
  • Fix bugs and keep the service working

We don’t sell your data, show targeted ads or use your content to train AI models.

What other people can see

Wotri is a social app, so most of what you post is visible to other users.

  • Public: your username, display name, bio, city, profile photo, ratings, rankings, reviews, photos, comments, reactions, public lists, and who you follow and who follows you.
  • Only you: your email address, password, location, private lists, and the accounts you block or report.

You won’t see content from people you block. Anything you post publicly may still be seen or saved by others before you delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Edit or delete content: change your profile, ratings, reviews, lists and photos anytime in the app.
  • Delete your account: go to Settings and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, ratings, reviews, comments, photos and other content.
  • Location and photos: you can turn off location, camera or photo access anytime in your phone’s settings. The app still works without them.
  • Block and report: block anyone from their profile, and report reviews, comments or accounts that break our rules.
  • Ask us: you can ask for a copy of your data or ask us to correct or delete it by emailing us (see Contact). Depending on where you live, such as California or the EU, you may have further rights, and we honor those requests.

How long we keep data and how we protect it

We keep your information while your account is open and delete it when you delete your account.

  • Restaurant, dish and menu details you add (names, prices, descriptions and addresses) become part of Wotri’s shared listings and stay after you leave, without your name on them. Photos you added, including restaurant photos and scanned menu pages, are deleted with your account.
  • We may keep reports and records of rule-breaking for as long as needed to keep the app safe.
  • Menu photos you scan are sent to Anthropic only to read the dishes. The pages are then saved to the restaurant’s photos as its menu, until you or we remove them.

Your data is sent over encrypted connections, and database rules limit each account to its own private data. No system is perfectly secure, so we can’t promise that data will never be exposed, but we will tell you if a breach affects you.

Children

Wotri is not meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. For important changes, we will also tell you in the app before they take effect.

Questions or requests about your privacy: support@wotri.app.
