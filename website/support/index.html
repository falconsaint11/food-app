Support

We usually reply within two days.

Contact us

Email support@wotri.app with your question, a bug or an idea. If something isn't working, tell us your Wotri username and what you were doing when it happened.

Delete your account

In the app, open your Profile, tap Settings, then Delete account. This permanently removes your account, reviews, photos, lists and comments. If you can't sign in, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

Report a post or a person

Tap ⋯ on any review or comment and choose Report. From the same ⋯ menu, on a post or on someone's profile, you can block them so you no longer see each other's posts. You can unblock people in Settings.

A restaurant or dish is missing or wrong

Search for the restaurant in the Log tab to add it, and add missing dishes from its page. To fix a wrong name or menu, email us and we'll update it.
